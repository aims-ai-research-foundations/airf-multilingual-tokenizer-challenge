01 · The challenge

Make every token count.

Tokenizers quietly shape context length, memory use, compute and cost. A design that is efficient for English can splinter Yoruba diacritics or Amharic script into far too many pieces.

Your task is to build one tokenizer that works well across all six—not six separate tokenizers.

02 · The metric

Lower is better.
Fairness is built in.

We measure how many tokens your tokenizer needs per character, normalize against the official baseline, then give every language equal weight.

1

Fertility per language

Fl = tokens ÷ characters
2

Normalize to baseline

Nl = Fl ÷ Fl baseline
3

Average six languages

Score = Σ Nl ÷ 6
0.8020% better0.9010% better1.00baseline>1.00keep going

Official ranking uses score only. Standardized throughput is reported separately in characters per second.

08 · FAQ

Good questions,
short answers.

Do I need a GPU?+

No. Tokenizer training is lightweight enough for a laptop or free Google Colab.

Can I build six separate tokenizers?+

No. The submitted JSON must contain one tokenizer that handles all six languages.

Can I use extra training data?+

Yes, if its license permits your use and you document it. Hidden or leaked evaluation text is prohibited.

Does speed change my score?+

No. Score alone determines ranking. Speed is measured on common hardware and shown as useful context.

When does the competition open?+

This is the complete technical prototype. Dates, contacts and any prize terms must be announced before launch.
